Privacy Notice

Comprehensive privacy notice under Mexico’s Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP), published in the Diario Oficial de la Federación on March 20, 2025. This English version is a courtesy translation; if there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

I. Identity and address of the data controller

Dr. Francisco Javier Cisneros Oliva, a physician specializing in orthopedics and traumatology, Mexican professional license (cédula profesional) 12294474, is responsible for processing your personal data. For the purposes of this notice, his addresses are his clinics:

  • Guadalajara: Av. Patria 1355, 32nd floor, office 3225, Hospital Ángeles Andares, Zapopan, Jalisco, 45116, Mexico.
  • Tijuana: Av. Paseo del Centenario 9580, 27th floor, New City Medical Plaza, Zona Urbana Río Tijuana, Baja California, 22010, Mexico.

COFEPRIS advertising notice: No. 2402022002A00211.

Advertising for Dr. Francisco Cisneros Oliva’s medical services, including advertising on this website and in digital campaigns in Mexico and abroad, is carried out under this permit from Mexico’s Federal Commission for the Protection against Sanitary Risks (COFEPRIS).

II. Personal data we collect

To fulfill the purposes of this notice, we may collect the following categories of data:

  • Identification: full name, date of birth, sex, CURP (Mexican population registry number) and official ID.
  • Contact: address, phone number, WhatsApp number and email.
  • Sensitive health data: medical history, symptoms, diagnoses, imaging (X-rays, MRIs, CT scans), medications, and medical, surgical and family history.
  • Billing and payment: tax information and the information needed to issue invoices and receipts.
  • Browsing: technical information obtained through cookies when you visit this website (see section VIII).

Sensitive health data is handled in strict confidence, only with your express consent and under the administrative, technical and physical security measures required by the LFPDPPP, in addition to medical professional confidentiality.

III. Purposes of processing

Primary purposes (necessary for the doctor-patient relationship):

  • Providing specialized spine care, including evaluation, diagnosis, treatment and follow-up.
  • Creating and keeping your medical record in accordance with Mexican standard NOM-004-SSA3-2012.
  • Scheduling, confirming and following up on appointments, tests and procedures.
  • Issuing prescriptions, test orders, invoices and receipts.
  • Contacting you or your representative to follow up on your treatment.

Secondary purposes (not necessary for medical care):

  • Sending you information about services, news and educational content related to spine health.
  • Conducting satisfaction surveys about the care you received.

If you do not want your data used for the secondary purposes, you can tell us at any time through the channels described in section V. Declining will not affect the medical care you receive.

IV. Data transfers

Your personal data is not transferred to third parties without your consent, except in the cases allowed by the LFPDPPP, such as:

  • Health, judicial or administrative authorities that require it under the law.
  • Hospitals, laboratories, imaging centers or other health professionals when necessary for your diagnosis or treatment.
  • Insurance companies, only when you ask for your care to be handled through your policy.

Providers that supply us with technology services (for example, messaging or website analytics) process information on our behalf and are required to keep it confidential.

V. ARCO rights and how to exercise them

You have the right to Access your personal data, Rectify it if it is inaccurate or incomplete, Cancel it when you believe it is not needed for any of the purposes described, and Object to its processing for specific purposes (ARCO rights).

Send your request on WhatsApp to the relevant clinic:

  • Guadalajara: +52 33 1172 8391
  • Tijuana: +52 664 159 6633

Your request must include:

  • Your full name and a way to send you our response.
  • A copy of a valid official ID or, if applicable, that of your legal representative and the document proving their authority to represent you.
  • A clear description of the right you want to exercise and the data it concerns.
  • Any document that helps locate your data.

We will respond within a maximum of 20 business days from receipt of your request. If the request is granted, it will take effect within 15 business days after our response.

Some medical record data must be kept for the period set by health regulations, so in those cases cancellation may not be possible right away.

VI. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw the consent you gave for the processing of your personal data through the same channels described in section V. Please note that, because of legal obligations, in some cases we may not be able to stop processing immediately.

VII. Options to limit the use or disclosure of your data

In addition to the channels in section V, you can sign up for the Public Registry to Avoid Advertising (REPEP) run by PROFECO, Mexico’s consumer protection agency, to limit the use of your data for advertising.

IX. Notice for U.S. visitors and California residents

Medical care is provided in Mexico and is governed by Mexican law. Because we also advertise to people who live in the United States, particularly in California, we include the following information to comply with the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and, voluntarily, with the rights recognized by the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Information we collect through the website and our campaigns:

  • Identifiers: name, phone or WhatsApp number and email, when you contact us.
  • Internet activity: pages visited, interaction with the site, browser and device type, and IP address, obtained through cookies.
  • Approximate location: city or region derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise location.
  • Sensitive health information: only what you choose to share when you request an appointment or during your care. It is used only to provide you with medical care and never for advertising or profiling.

Third parties that may collect information on the site: Google (Analytics, Ads and Maps) and Meta (WhatsApp, when you start a conversation). These third parties may collect information about your activity over time and across different websites, under their own privacy policies.

Sale and sharing of data: we do not sell your personal information. Advertising-measurement cookies could be considered “sharing” information for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law; you can turn them off at any time. We do not share your information with third parties for their own direct marketing purposes (California “Shine the Light,” Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.83).

Browser privacy signals: we honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as a request not to sell or share your information: when your browser sends it, we automatically turn off advertising cookies, even if you accepted them before. The site does not respond to “Do Not Track” (DNT) signals, because there is no common standard for interpreting them; we recommend using GPC.

Your rights. Although the CCPA/CPRA may not apply to us because of the thresholds set by law, we recognize the following rights for any U.S. resident:

  • Know what personal information we collect, what we use it for and who we share it with, and get a copy.
  • Ask us to delete it, except information we must keep by law, such as medical records.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your information (Do Not Sell or Share).
  • Limit the use of your sensitive information to what is necessary to provide you with medical care.
  • Not be treated differently for exercising any of these rights.

You can send your request on WhatsApp to +52 33 1172 8391 o +52 664 159 6633, directly or through an authorized agent. We will verify your identity before providing or deleting information and will respond within 45 days, which may be extended once when necessary, in which case we will let you know.

Retention: we keep contact information for as long as needed to handle your request and follow up on your care; medical records are kept for the period required by Mexican health regulations. Minors: the site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information online.

Summary for U.S. and California residents

Medical care is provided in Mexico under Mexican law. Through this website and our ads we collect identifiers (name, phone/WhatsApp, email), internet activity via cookies, approximate location from your IP address, and only the health information you choose to share to receive care, which is never used for advertising. Google (Analytics, Ads, Maps) and Meta (WhatsApp) may collect information about your activity over time and across websites. We do not sell personal information. To submit a request, message us on WhatsApp at +52 33 1172 8391 or +52 664 159 6633; we will verify your identity and respond within 45 days.

X. Changes to this privacy notice

This notice may be updated because of legal changes or changes to our services or privacy practices. Any change will be published on the Spanish version of this page, www.doctordecolumna.com/aviso-privacidad/, and reflected in this translation.

XI. Competent authority

If you believe your right to personal data protection has been violated, you may contact Mexico’s Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (Ministry of Anti-Corruption and Good Governance), the authority responsible for enforcing the LFPDPPP since March 2025.
